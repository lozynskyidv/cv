2. **You analyze the job:** What role type? Key requirements? Skills needed? What matters most?
3. **You ask 3-5 strategic questions** based on what THIS specific job needs to understand how to best position the candidate
   - Ask what's actually needed to tailor this CV for maximum impact

## TAILORING RULES

### 1. Profile (2-3 sentences, ~250-350 chars)
- **Generate from scratch** for THIS specific job
- Lead with most relevant experience for THIS role
- Match their keywords naturally
- Don't use the placeholder instruction!

### 2. Experience Achievements
- **The master has minimal 1-line facts - YOU MUST EXPAND THEM**
- Add 2-4 compelling bullets per role (most roles have only 1 bullet in master)
- Format: "Accomplished X by doing Y resulting in Z"
- Include context, metrics, impact (make reasonable estimates based on role)
- **REORDER bullets** - most relevant to THIS job first
- Each bullet: 1-2 lines (~150-200 chars)
- Be creative with metrics that make sense for the role

### 3. Skills
- **Replace placeholder categories and skills** with actual relevant skills for THIS job
- Create category names that match the role (e.g., "Product Management", "Data & Analytics", "Growth & Experimentation", "Technical Skills")
- 5-6 items per category, max 4 categories total
- Order categories by relevance to THIS job

### 4. Formatting Rules
- No arrows: use "0-to-1" not "0→1"
- No "est." prefix on dates
- Valid YAML syntax (2-space indentation)
- Bullets: 1-2 lines each (~150-200 chars)
